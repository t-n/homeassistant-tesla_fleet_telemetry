# AI policy

This project welcomes AI-assisted development. Contributors remain responsible for every change they submit.

This policy follows the spirit of the [Open Home Foundation AI Policy](https://developers.home-assistant.io/docs/ai_policy).

## Autonomous agents

Do **not** use autonomous agents to open issues, pull requests, or comments on this repository. A human must review, understand, and be able to explain every contribution before it is submitted.

## Code and documentation

AI tools may help write code and docs. Before submitting:

- Review every change for correctness and security.
- Be able to explain the change in your own words.
- Do not submit unreviewed AI output.

## Communication

Do not have tools post unreviewed content on your behalf. Keep responses to the minimum needed to communicate intent.

If you include context from an AI interaction, put it in a quote block, disclose that it is AI-generated, and add your own commentary. Do not paste long unreviewed snippets.

Using AI to improve grammar or clarity of text you wrote is fine.

## Secrets

Never paste OAuth tokens, client secrets, refresh tokens, VINs, private keys, or private hostnames into issues, PRs, or AI prompts that leave your machine.
